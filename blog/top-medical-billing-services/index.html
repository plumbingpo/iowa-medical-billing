2

AdvancedMD

AdvancedMD offers an integrated suite of cloud-based solutions for medical practices, including practice management, electronic health records (EHR), and medical billing services. They aim to streamline workflows and improve financial performance for healthcare providers.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Specialty Clinics (e.g., Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics), Mental Health, Urgent Care, Physical Therapy.

Pricing Model: Typically a percentage of collections (e.g., 4-8%) or a per-provider/per-month fee for their full suite. Specific billing service pricing would be a component of this.

  • ✅ Integrated EHR/PM/Billing system can lead to seamless data flow and efficiency.
  • ✅ Strong reporting and analytics capabilities to track financial performance.
  • ⚠️ Can be a more expensive option, especially for smaller practices, due to the comprehensive nature of their platform.
3

Experian Health (formerly Passport Health Communications)

Experian Health, a division of Experian, provides a wide range of healthcare revenue cycle management solutions, including patient access, claims management, and payment integrity. While not solely a billing company, their services directly support and often integrate with medical billing operations for Iowa practices.

Specialties Served: Hospitals, Large Health Systems, Physician Practices of all sizes, Ambulatory Surgery Centers.

Pricing Model: Subscription-based for software solutions, transaction-based for specific services (e.g., eligibility checks), or project-based for consulting. Exact billing service pricing would be custom.

  • ✅ Leverages Experian's data analytics capabilities for improved patient identification and payment prediction.
  • ✅ Offers solutions that address the entire revenue cycle, not just claims submission.
  • ⚠️ Their suite of services can be complex and may require significant integration efforts for some practices.
4

eClinicalWorks

eClinicalWorks is a leading provider of ambulatory clinical and practice management systems, including comprehensive medical billing services. They offer an all-in-one solution designed to help practices manage patient care and financial operations efficiently.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, Cardiology, Gastroenterology, Orthopedics, Urgent Care.

Pricing Model: Often a percentage of collections (estimated 5-8%) or a combination of monthly software fees and billing service fees. Pricing can vary based on volume and services selected.

  • ✅ Integrated EHR and billing system reduces manual data entry and errors.
  • ✅ Strong focus on interoperability and patient engagement tools.
  • ⚠️ Some users report a steep learning curve for their comprehensive platform.
5

CareCloud

CareCloud provides cloud-based practice management, electronic health record (EHR), and medical billing solutions. They focus on helping practices optimize their financial and operational performance through intuitive technology and expert services.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Mental Health, Urgent Care, and various other specialties.

Pricing Model: Typically a percentage of collections (estimated 5-9%) for their full RCM services, or a per-provider/per-month fee for software with optional billing services.

  • ✅ Modern, user-friendly interface for their platform.
  • ✅ Dedicated account management and support for billing clients.
  • ⚠️ Can be on the higher end of pricing for smaller practices compared to purely billing-focused companies.
6

Medical Business Management (MBM)

Medical Business Management (MBM) is a revenue cycle management company that offers comprehensive medical billing services. They focus on maximizing reimbursement and improving financial health for healthcare providers across various specialties.

Specialties Served: Family Practice, Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Chiropractic, Physical Therapy, Mental Health.

Pricing Model: Primarily a percentage of collections (estimated 4-7%). They may also offer custom solutions with flat fees for specific tasks.

  • ✅ Strong emphasis on reducing claim denials and accelerating payments.
  • ✅ Often provides personalized service with dedicated billing specialists.
  • ⚠️ May not offer an integrated EHR system, requiring practices to use their own or integrate with MBM's billing platform.
7

Optum360 (a division of Optum, UnitedHealth Group)

Optum360 provides comprehensive revenue cycle management services, including medical billing, coding, and denial management, for healthcare providers of all sizes. They leverage extensive industry expertise and technology to optimize financial performance.

Specialties Served: Hospitals, Large Health Systems, Physician Groups, Ambulatory Surgical Centers, Specialty Clinics.

Pricing Model: Customized based on the scope of services, volume, and complexity. Typically a percentage of collections (estimated 3-8%) or a fee-for-service model for specific RCM components.

  • ✅ Backed by the vast resources and data analytics capabilities of Optum/UnitedHealth Group.
  • ✅ Offers highly sophisticated and scalable solutions for complex healthcare organizations.
  • ⚠️ Can be a more expensive and enterprise-level solution, potentially less suitable for very small independent practices.
